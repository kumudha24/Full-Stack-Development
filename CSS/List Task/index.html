1. Fill the kettle with water and replace on the stand.

  2. Switch on the power button.

  3. Wait approx. 3 minutes. In the meantime, take a large mug and place
    the teabag in the cup.

  4. When the kettle has boiled, fill the mug with boiling water.Do not leave
    water to stand for more than one minute, or you must boil again.

  5. Leave for 3-4 minutes, stirring occasionally.

  6. Remove teabag.

  7. Add milk according to taste.

  8. Serve immediately.
